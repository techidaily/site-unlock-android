Now, ensure that HTC Sync Manager is installed on the PC.

Step 3: From the bootloader page, select your device using the drop down option as shown in the picture below.

![htc unlock bootloader](https://images.wondershare.com/drfone/article/2016/12/14822578344435.jpg)

![htc unlock bootloader](https://images.wondershare.com/drfone/article/2016/12/14822589729198.jpg)

![htc unlock bootloader](https://images.wondershare.com/drfone/article/2016/12/14822588941681.jpg)

Step 7: In the command prompt window, type “fastboot devices” and press enter. Honor Play 8T  will show up in the command prompt.

Note: The drivers have to be installed correctly to see the Honor Play 8T device in the command prompt. So, if the Honor Play 8T device does not show up, reinstall HTC Sync Manager and try again after restarting the computer.
