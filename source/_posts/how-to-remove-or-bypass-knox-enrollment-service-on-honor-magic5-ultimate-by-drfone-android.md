## Part 2: How To Remove Knox Enrollment Service on Your Honor Magic5 Ultimate

Here’s a step-by-step guide to removing itfrom the Admin’s Portal:

- **Step 1:** Sign in to the Samsung Knox Portal using administrative credentials.
- **Step 2:** Navigate to **Samsung Knox Mobile Enrollment**, then click **Devices.**
- **Step 3:** Select the Honor Magic5 Ultimate device in question and click **Actions.**
- **Step 4:** Choose the **Clear Profile** option to remove all profiles from the Honor Magic5 Ultimate device. This action will disassociate the Honor Magic5 Ultimate device from any existing Knox configurations.

Be aware that this option might not be available on all devices, depending on how Knox was configured.
